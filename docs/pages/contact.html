My name is Mel Thill and I have recently graduated with a MSc in Software Development from Coventry University. Professionally, I am most strongly interested in UI/UX research and design. So much so that both my final degree projects focussed heavily on those subjects. Check out my portfolio if you want to know more!

When I am not designing software or writing code, I love to draw and sometimes I even get commissioned to create custom pieces. On the whole I am a very artsy person and some of my other hobbies include linocut, cross stitch and crochet.
